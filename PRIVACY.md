**Last Updated:** November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **[Bot Name]** ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects, uses, and stores your information when you use our service on Discord.

We only collect data that you voluntarily provide to the Bot when you choose to opt in and use its features:

Your information is used strictly to operate the Bot's core feature:
* Routing and delivering messages or information from one opted-in user to the intended opted-in recipient.
* Verifying that both the sender and the recipient have consented and opted in to the service before relaying any information.

* **Mutual Opt-In:** Information is only relayed between users who have both explicitly opted in and consented to use the service.

* Your data is stored securely only for as long as you remain opted in to the service.
* Relayed messages are processed solely to deliver them to the recipient and are not permanently logged or sold.

Because all data is provided voluntarily, you are in full control of your information:
* **Opt Out & Delete:** You can opt out of the service or request to have all of your stored data (your name, Discord ID, and saved contacts) permanently deleted at any time.
* **How to Request Deletion:** To remove your data or ask questions about this policy, please contact **[Your Discord Username / Support Server Link / Email Address]**.
